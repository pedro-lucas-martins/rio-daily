About

Sources

News: Agência Brasil, The Rio Times and Google News. Crime: ISP-RJ{% if zones.has_shootings %} and Fogo Cruzado{% endif %}. Neighbourhood borders: Rio City Hall. Map: OpenStreetMap. Flights: Google Flights through SerpApi. Price history compares one fixed trip with itself; other prices rotate across dates.

Method

Hot zones combine official reports (50%), shootings (25%) and recent news (25%); if a source is missing, the others are reweighted. Official reports are street robbery plus phone and pocket theft (weight {{ cfg.zones.theft_weight }}), counted over 12 months and compared with the same months a year earlier. Counts are not divided by residents, since visitors outnumber them in tourist areas, so large or busy areas tend to score higher.

Limits

News is machine translated and may contain errors: open the source. This is not emergency information or official advice. Deadly events and areas rising to red are reviewed by a person first and may appear late.
